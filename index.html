How to play

Three in a row. That's it.

  1. 1

    Take turns

    Players alternate placing X and O on the 3×3 grid.

  2. 2

    Line up three

    The first to get three marks in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins.

  3. 3

    Draw & rematch

    If the grid fills with no line, it's a draw. One tap and you're playing again.
